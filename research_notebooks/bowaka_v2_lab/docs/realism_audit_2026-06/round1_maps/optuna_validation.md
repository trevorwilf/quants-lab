## Purpose & data flow (how this subsystem fits the backtest pipeline)

This subsystem is the **gating layer** wrapped around a walk-forward Optuna study. Flow: `autoconfig.resolve_walkforward_config` probes the lake and pins `feed`+`simulation.mode` → `dispatcher.OptunaStudy.create` builds the named study (TPE, seed 1337) → `preflight.run_full_fold_preflight` refuses the study before any trial if folds lack DQ/quote/minute/PIT coverage → `dispatcher.run_bowaka_optimization_dispatch` (or `parallel.run_parallel_bowaka_optimization`) runs trials serial or process-parallel → `study_validity.evaluate_study_validity` declares the completed study scientifically valid/invalid → `evaluate_finalists.evaluate_finalists` re-runs top-K + incumbent in full artifact mode with holdout/stress/sensitivity → `stop_ship_checklist.evaluate_stop_ship` is the cheap pure promotion gate → `promotion_gates`/`promotion.suitability`/`promotion.checklist` produce the mechanical tier verdict. `holdout.score_final_holdout` is the single sanctioned read of the holdout window. `pit_universe` supplies the eligible-symbol denominators preflight uses.
